Ro-Bought

One retailer, one product, one unit, once. Your account, your checkout. The bot just clicks faster.

Before a drop: leave Chrome open with the retailer tab in the foreground, keep your computer awake and plugged in, and stay signed in to the retailer with a saved address and payment method. Extensions can't run while Chrome is closed or the machine is asleep, and they can't read Chrome's saved cards.
A run is active. Disarm it from the toolbar popup before changing settings.

Purchase

Ro-Bought will place the order by itself. It only does so when the order total is at or below your max, the cart holds exactly one unit of this product, and it hasn't already clicked "Place order" in this run. Do a dry run with "Stop one click short" ticked first.
In the store's currency, including tax and shipping. The bot won't buy above it. Required if the bot places the order.

Before a drop: empty your cart, and make sure your account has a saved address and payment method. Ro-Bought never types card details, passwords or codes.

Buttons

To teach Ro-Bought the exact buttons, open the store in a tab, click the 🤖 Ro-Bought icon → Teach buttons, and pick each button on its page. Add something to your cart and go as far as the final review page, but don't place the order. Taught buttons always win over the built-in ones.

    Saving asks Chrome for access to only the retailer's site. Any previously granted retailer is revoked.

    Google Alerts

    Hear about a drop or restock the moment it's announced. Google Alerts has no API, so Ro-Bought fills in the search for you and you click Create alert.

    Filled in from the product name. Edit it if you like.
    1. On Google Alerts, the search is already filled in (it's also on your clipboard).
    2. For alerts by email: click Create alert. Done.
    3. For alerts here, in Chrome: click Show options → Deliver to → RSS feed → Create alert. Then copy the RSS icon's link next to the alert and paste it below.
    Ro-Bought checks it every 30 minutes. Saving asks for access to Google Alerts feeds only.
