Ro-Bought

One retailer, one product, one unit, once. Your account, your checkout. The bot just clicks faster.

Before a drop: leave Chrome open with the retailer tab in the foreground, keep your computer awake and plugged in, and stay signed in to the retailer with a saved address and payment method. Extensions can't run while Chrome is closed or the machine is asleep, and they can't read Chrome's saved cards.
A run is active. Disarm it from the toolbar popup before changing settings.

Product

Negative fires early, positive fires late. 0 is usually right.
